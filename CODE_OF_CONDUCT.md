# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the `branding-skills` project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, nationality, language, or identity.

This project is built around branding and design work that regularly touches different markets and cultures (including Arabic and Egyptian/Arab-market references). We ask everyone involved to treat that material, and each other, with respect.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and design opinions
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on skills, references, and prompts
- Crediting sources properly when adding reference material (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`)
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Submitting reference material, examples, or prompts that copy another brand's actual logo, trademark, or copyrighted work instead of studying it as a reference
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in any public space when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue tagged `conduct`, or by contacting the maintainer directly via GitHub: [@mostafaibrahem847-sudo](https://github.com/mostafaibrahem847-sudo). All reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion.

The maintainer has the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior deemed inappropriate.
